# Shopymalls POC Image Assets

These 38 fictional, unbranded images were created with the built-in image-generation tool for the Shopymalls POC on 2026-09-08. They are optimized as WebP files at quality 82; the complete collection is approximately 6.3 MB.

## Art direction and prompt set

Shared direction for malls, stores, and events:

> Premium realistic editorial photography for a dark, image-led Jakarta shopping app. Contemporary Indonesian setting, near-black and charcoal surfaces, warm architectural lighting, tropical details, polished commercial finish, and crop-safe composition. No readable signage, logos, embedded text, watermarks, UI, recognizable trademarks, or close identifiable faces.

Shared direction for products:

> Ultra-realistic high-end studio product photography. Single centered fictional product arrangement on a matte near-black surface, square crop-safe composition, warm sculptural light, natural materials, and at most one restrained lime accent. No branding, labels, letters, numbers, watermarks, hands, or UI.

Shared direction for avatars:

> Natural premium editorial headshot of a fictional contemporary Jakarta professional. Square head-and-shoulders framing, softly blurred charcoal interior, flattering warm light, realistic skin texture, and a confident, friendly expression. No logos, text, watermark, or UI.

The mall map prompt requested a fictional top-down level with a central oval atrium, looping corridors, approximately twenty retail units, anchor spaces, entrances, elevators, escalators, and restrooms, rendered in charcoal with warm-white lines and restrained lime highlights, without labels or real-world geometry.

## Usage notes

- Keep names, discounts, prices, dates, and other Indonesian/English copy in the UI rather than baking them into images.
- Mall and event images are prepared for wide cards; product and avatar images are square; store images support either 4:3 or cropped wide cards.
- The fictional names in this manifest are suggested seed-data names and do not represent real businesses or partnerships.
